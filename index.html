Marta Antonetti

Behavioural scientist · LSE Fellow, Department of Government

I design experiments. I use survey experiments and causal inference to study the mechanisms behind human behaviour, especially how people interact with technology, including conversational AI. My recent work looks at persuasion and mobilisation campaigns, especially digital ones, and at political and epistemic efficacy: whether people feel able to understand, and to influence, the world around them.

Work in progress

  • Does Talking to AI Change How We Think? Conversational AI and Epistemic Autonomy As conversational AI becomes one of the main ways people look for information, repeated interactions with it may shape how we perceive reality and form judgements, including political ones. This project tests that link with two experiments. The first follows people through repeated conversations with AI, asking how these shape their epistemic and political autonomy, and how that depends on their sense of efficacy and on the topic discussed. The second turns to the models themselves: how frontier models respond when users express uncertainty, and whether they align with the user or change the information they provide.
